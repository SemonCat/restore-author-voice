# Restore author voice

Write prose that sounds like its author thinking clearly. Preserve evidence and genre before changing style.

## Modes

- **Diagnose:** locate changes that would recover meaning or voice; do not rewrite.
- **Cleanup:** remove obvious AI artifacts with minimal structural change.
- **Rewrite:** rebuild a supplied draft while preserving facts, stance, uncertainty, and genre.
- **Draft:** write new prose only from supplied or verified material.

## Workflow

1. **Set the boundary.** Identify audience, genre, target language, mode, and editing strength.
   **Complete when:** the output form and editing boundary are clear.

2. **Lock protected content.** Preserve names, numbers, dates, links, quotations, requirements, legal or financial terms, chronology, and stated positions. Separate fact, inference, opinion, and quotation.
   **Complete when:** every important claim has a source and every protected item has a disposition.

3. **Establish author evidence.** Prefer the draft, explicit instructions, then a profile built from multiple attributable samples. Treat profiles as style evidence, never task facts. Without author evidence, keep the genre natural without inventing a persona.
   **Complete when:** 3–7 voice or meaning anchors are traceable to evidence.

4. **Diagnose the loss.** Read [references/diagnostic-lens.md](references/diagnostic-lens.md). For de-AI or cleanup work, also read [references/ai-patterns.md](references/ai-patterns.md). Ask at most three questions, only when answers would materially change the result.
   **Complete when:** each planned change fixes a located problem instead of chasing a human score.

5. **Write in two passes.** Restore actors, specifics, constraints, costs, decisions, and honest uncertainty. Then tune wording, rhythm, paragraphs, and formatting to the author and genre. For Traditional Chinese aimed at Taiwan, read [references/zh-tw.md](references/zh-tw.md).
   **Complete when:** meaning and evidence strength have not drifted, and the prose has not become a replacement template.

6. **Verify and deliver.** Re-read against the source, run the author-swap test, and use [references/evaluation.md](references/evaluation.md) when changing this skill or handling high-risk prose. Deliver usable copy by default.
   **Complete when:** protected content survives, new details are sourced, and author-specific passages fail a plausible author swap.

## Hard guardrails

- Optimize for truthful, effective prose, not AI-detector scores.
- Add no invented experience, quotation, number, emotion, relationship, or opinion.
- Treat pattern lists as signals, not bans. Keep passive voice, adverbs, dashes, repetition, or three-item lists when the genre and author support them.
- Preserve necessary neutrality in specifications, legal text, compliance copy, tables, schemas, and verbatim quotation.
- Build profiles only from attributable samples. Use [templates/author-profile.md](templates/author-profile.md); do not infer sensitive traits.
