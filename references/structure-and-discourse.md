# Structure and discourse

Use this reference before sentence-level editing when the defect may live in narrative architecture, section order, paragraph function, or pacing. Diagnose first; do not manufacture irregularity for its own sake.

## Fiction and narrative

Inspect these decision groups:

- **Theme:** Does the narrator explain what events already show? Keep a deliberate explicit statement; cut a generic lesson or convert it to action, image, or unresolved consequence.
- **Causal shape:** Does every beat cause the next in one clean track? Keep coherent causality, but inspect absent subplots, offstage causes, overly scheduled reconciliation, and every setup paying off.
- **Resolution:** Does the protagonist always decide, grow, understand, and accept? Change only the leg that weakens this story; partial, external, open, or catastrophic outcomes are options, not requirements.
- **Time and revelation:** Is all context front-loaded and chronological? Move information only when later disclosure creates useful recontextualization.
- **Emotion:** Is every feeling rendered as bodily sensation, weather, or metaphor? Mix behavior, plain naming, embodied sensation, and ambiguity; reserve heightened rendering for actual peaks.
- **Characters:** Are introductions descriptive, every character connected, and every relationship warm? Let action, dialogue, social distance, and structured disagreement do real work when the premise supports them.
- **World and reader:** Prefer accurate named references, meaningful locations, and occasional reader presence over vague allusion. Never invent a reference to satisfy the check.

Select a few relevant moves for the piece. Do not invert every signal or force a rarity move into a draft that does not need one.

## Paragraph and section flow

1. List the implicit question answered by each paragraph or scene.
2. Flag a purely administrative sequence such as setup → reason → consequence → reflection, repeated at every level.
3. Check for missing moves that the material supports: comparison, contradiction, verification, digression, or delayed setup.
4. Extract each paragraph's first sentence. A perfect stand-alone summary may expose template-shaped flow; gaps are acceptable when the full paragraphs earn them.
5. Inspect the middle third. Give depth to the actual complication, evidence, or failed path instead of accelerating through it.
6. Check positional sameness: uniform paragraph lengths, every key line at the end, every list containing three items, identical transitions, or emphasis spread evenly.

Reorder or replace an underlying move before paraphrasing it. Vary structure because meaning demands it, not to create noise.

## Depth decision

- **Cleanup:** preserve architecture unless a small move removes an obvious reflection tail, duplicate summary, or misplaced setup.
- **Rewrite:** extract protected facts and intent, diagnose all layers, then rebuild deepest-first.
- **Draft:** decide narrative architecture or section logic before polishing sentences.
- **Diagnose:** report the layer, quoted evidence, reader cost, and smallest credible repair; apply nothing.
