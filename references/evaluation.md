# Evaluation

## Invariants

- Preserve names, numbers, dates, links, quotations, requirements, and legal or financial terms unless correction is authorized.
- Source every added fact.
- Preserve evidence strength, genre, and audience.
- Do not replace one cliché with another.
- Report no detector score or “human percentage”.

## Change

Remove generic era-setting openings, empty “not just a tool but a revolution” claims, unsourced precise metrics, empty three-step exhortations, generic optimistic conclusions, and “希望這對你有幫助” from article body. Mark missing facts or sources instead of inventing them.

## Preserve

Keep real decision conditions, sourced metrics, necessary refund/legal/compliance language, useful technical contrasts, and quoted or official terminology.

## Mode gates

Diagnose does not rewrite. Cleanup changes as little as possible. Rewrite may restructure but passes every invariant. Draft adds no unsupported fact, experience, or opinion.

Finish by comparing source and output, running the author-swap test, deleting unsupported personality, and confirming required neutral passages stayed neutral.
